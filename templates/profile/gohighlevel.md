# Profile template: GoHighLevel and CRM automation

Orientation for `/profile`, never text to paste as it stands. It follows the rules in
`references/profile.md`, a count of the newest Upwork postings on 5 October 2026 and
eight successful public profiles. Every `[slot]` is filled from `context/me.md`. A slot
with no evidence behind it is cut, never filled with a guess. Upwork's skill is called
HighLevel, not GoHighLevel.

## What clients type (measured 5 October 2026)

- **GoHighLevel:** the 20 newest postings spread over 19 hours, a thinner market than SEO
  or Google Ads. 10 hourly, 10 fixed. Posted hourly ranges had a median of $19 to $55,
  the highest top end of the lanes measured; fixed budgets were small tasks, median $17.50.
- **Experience:** 11 of 20 asked for expert, none for entry level.
- **What the work is:** automation in 13 of 20 postings, CRM in 11, email in 8, funnels in
  7, SMS in 2. SEO appeared in 1 and Google Ads in none, so this profile stands on its own
  rather than beside an SEO or ads profile.
- **Industries in titles:** gyms and fitness 2, roofing, small business, digital
  products, loyalty programmes; the snippets add home services, agencies and coaches.

## What successful GoHighLevel profiles do (8 measured)

Eight public Top Rated Plus profiles, read 5 October 2026. Their signals are weaker than
in the other lanes: a median of 21 jobs and $20 an hour, against clients posting up to
$55. The lane is young, so a member with a clear offer can stand out quickly.

- **Transfers:** GoHighLevel in the title (8 of 8); funnels (8 of 8); SMS or email
  follow-up (7 of 8); AI chatbots or voice agents (5 of 8); Zapier, Make or n8n named (4
  of 8).
- **Nobody does it:** a client result number in the overview (0 of 8), the client's
  problem in the first 250 characters (0 of 8), a price (0 of 8), a disqualification line
  (0 of 8). Every one of those is free to take.

## The other fields

- **Rate:** set from the member's own work and proof, never from the posted ranges
  above, which describe what clients offered on one day.

  employer's customer got.
- **Intro video:** 30 to 90 seconds on the lost-lead problem, what the system does, and
  the close.
