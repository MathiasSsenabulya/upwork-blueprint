# Profile template: Google Ads with conversion

Orientation for `/profile`, never text to paste as it stands. It follows the rules in
`references/profile.md` and a count of the newest Upwork postings on 5 October 2026.
Every `[slot]` is filled from `context/me.md`. A slot with no evidence behind it is
cut, never filled with a guess, and no number goes in that the evidence sections
cannot back.

## What clients type (measured 5 October 2026)

- **Google Ads:** the 20 newest postings arrived within 7 hours. 12 hourly, 8 fixed;
  posted hourly ranges had a median of $10 to $25, fixed budgets a median of $362.50.
  No posting asked for entry level.
- **Title words:** Google Ads 14, Meta 8, Specialist 6, Campaign 4, Conversion
  Tracking 3, Setup 3, Google Ads Audit 2, Fix 2.
- **Conversion belongs here.** 10 of the 20 postings named conversion tracking, Tag
  Manager, Analytics or a pixel. Clients who pay for clicks ask whether the clicks pay.
- **Meta comes along in postings, not in winning profiles.** 8 of 20 titles asked for
  Meta and Google together, yet 7 of 8 successful profiles sell Google Ads alone. Name
  Meta only when the member runs it, and never lead with it.
- **Conversion tracking is the steadiest term:** 4.5 new jobs a day on 5 October, three
  of them still under ten proposals after two days.

## What successful Google Ads profiles do (8 measured)

Eight public Top Rated Plus Google Ads profiles, read 5 October 2026, chosen by Google
rank, so not a random sample. This lane pays best of the four measured (median $89 an
hour) and writes the most prose.

- **Transfers:** one channel, Google Ads alone (7 of 8); conversion tracking named (6
  of 8); a free first step as the close, an audit, a review or a call (5 of 8); mostly
  prose rather than emoji lists (5 of 8).
- **Transfers once there is proof:** a money number early, ad spend, revenue or ROAS (7
  of 8, four inside the first 250 characters); numbers in portfolio titles (6 of 8).
  Without verified results, the opening carries the problem instead.
- **Nobody does it:** a price (0 of 8), a guarantee (0 of 8). A disqualification line
  appears in 2 of 8, the only lane where any did.

## Title

Pick one shape and change it to what the member really does. 55 to 70 characters, a
position (role, then who it is for), not a stack of keywords.

```text
Google Ads Specialist | PPC & Tracking for Lead Generation
```

Other shapes, only when the member brings that expertise: `Google Ads Auditor | PPC Account Audits for Lead Generation` for audit-led work,
`Google Ads & Meta Ads Specialist | PPC for Local Businesses` for members who run both.

## The other fields

- **Rate:** set from the member's own work and proof, never from the posted ranges
  above, which describe what clients offered on one day.
- **Employment history:** agency or in-house paid media work counts; say what that
  employer's customer got.
- **Intro video:** 30 to 90 seconds on the problem above, tracking first, and the close.
