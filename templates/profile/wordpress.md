# Profile template: WordPress sites, fixes and care

Orientation for `/profile`, never text to paste as it stands. It follows the rules in
`references/profile.md`, a count of the newest Upwork postings on 5 October 2026 and
eight successful public profiles. Every `[slot]` is filled from `context/me.md`. A slot
with no evidence behind it is cut, never filled with a guess.

## What clients type (measured 5 October 2026)

- **Web design:** the 20 newest postings arrived within 4.5 hours, 10 hourly and 10
  fixed. Posted hourly ranges had a median of $19 to $30; fixed budgets a median of $150.
  10 of the 20 carried Graphic Design: half this market wants a designer, not a site.
- **WordPress:** 10 postings within 4 hours, mostly maintenance, fixes, migrations,
  security and WooCommerce. Fixed budgets ran from $5 to $1,200 with a median of $15.
- **So:** many small jobs. They pay only for a member who can finish one quickly, which
  is why the promise below is speed and care, not design.
- **Better-paid neighbours:** Webflow (7.8 new jobs a day, $300 fixed median) and
  Framer (4.1 a day, $300) paid more than WordPress on the same day. A member who works
  in them uses the Webflow title shape below.

## What successful WordPress profiles do (8 measured)

Eight public Top Rated Plus web profiles, read 5 October 2026, chosen by Google rank.

- **Transfers:** WordPress or Elementor in the title (6 of 8); SEO bundled with the
  build (6 of 8); landing pages or conversion named (6 of 8); ongoing maintenance offered
  (5 of 8); a call to action (7 of 8).
- **Does not transfer yet:** opening on years, reviews or "top 1%" (most of the 8).
- **Nobody does it:** a client result with a number (7 of 8 have none), a price (0 of 8),
  a disqualification line (0 of 8). A member who can show one measured result already
  stands out.

## The edge to sell

A member who runs Claude Code against a client's WordPress site through MCP fixes, updates
and extends it in a fraction of the usual time. Sell what that makes possible, never the
tool: a fix inside 24 hours, every change logged, a monthly care plan at a set price.
Clients read "AI" as a risk to their site; they read "fixed today, with a log" as relief.
Promise the window only when the member can keep it.

## Title

Pick one shape and change it to what the member really does. 55 to 70 characters, a
position (role, then who it is for), not a stack of keywords.

```text
WordPress Developer | Elementor & WooCommerce Sites for Shops
```

Other shapes, only when the member brings that expertise: `WordPress Maintenance Expert | Fixes and Security for Small Sites` for fix and
care work, `Webflow Developer | Landing Pages for Growing Businesses` for Webflow members.
