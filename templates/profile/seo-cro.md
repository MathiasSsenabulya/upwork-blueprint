# Profile template: SEO with conversion

Orientation for `/profile`, never text to paste as it stands. It follows the rules in
`references/profile.md` and a count of the newest Upwork postings on 5 October 2026.
Every `[slot]` is filled from `context/me.md`. A slot with no evidence behind it is
cut, never filled with a guess, and no number goes in that the evidence sections
cannot back.

## What clients type (measured 5 October 2026)

- **SEO:** the 20 newest postings arrived within 7 hours. 15 hourly, 5 fixed; posted
  hourly ranges had a median of $15 to $25. No posting asked for entry level.
- **Title words:** SEO 20, Specialist 9, Expert 4, Technical SEO 2, Shopify 2,
  Backlinks 2, GEO 2, Local SEO 1, Google Knowledge Panel 1.
- **Conversion is a separate, smaller market.** The 10 newest conversion-rate postings
  took 18 days to appear, mostly SaaS, Shopify and B2B, with posted hourly ranges at a
  median of $20 to $50. None of the 20 SEO titles named conversion, landing pages or
  funnels.
- **So:** SEO leads the title. Conversion is the second half of the promise, traffic
  that turns into calls, and its terms ride in the skills and the text.

## What successful SEO profiles do (8 measured)

Eight public Top Rated Plus SEO profiles, read 5 October 2026, chosen by Google rank, so
not a random sample. Their weight comes from 40 to 1,184 finished jobs a new member does
not have, so copy the shape that transfers and leave the rest.

- **Transfers:** titles at 65 to 70 characters (8 of 8); at least 14 skills filled (8 of
  8); AI search named, AEO, GEO or AI SEO (6 of 8).
- **Does not transfer yet:** opening on credential stats or jobs done (most of the 8),
  long emoji or checkmark lists (6 of 8). Without that history, the opening carries the
  client's problem and the one verified result instead, as below.
- **Nobody does it, so it stands out:** an industry in the title (0 of 8), a
  disqualification line (0 of 8), a price (1 of 8).

## Title

Pick one shape and change it to what the member really does. 55 to 70 characters, a
position (role, then who it is for), not a stack of keywords.

```text
SEO Expert | SEO Strategy & Rankings for Small Businesses
```

Every block must also be one of the skills below: the `/profile` gate refuses a title
word it cannot find there, and "CRO" is not an Upwork skill.

Other shapes, only when the member brings that expertise: `Technical SEO Auditor | Site Audits for Growing Websites` for audit-led work,
`Shopify SEO Expert | Technical SEO for Online Stores` for stores.
Shopify SEO had the steadier demand of the two on 5 October: 5.6 new jobs a day, three of
them still under ten proposals after a day, real fixed budgets of $1,000 to $2,000.

only when that entry backs it.

- `Google Business Profile rebuild for a [business type]`

## The other fields

- **Rate:** set from the member's own work and proof, never from the posted ranges
  above, which describe what clients offered on one day.
- **Employment history:** agency or in-house marketing work counts; say what that
  employer's customer got.
- **Intro video:** 30 to 90 seconds on the problem above, the two halves of the work,
  and the close.
